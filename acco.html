President PAUL Biya Accomplishments

1. Diplomacy and International Relations

  • Bakassi Peninsula Resolution:

Biya is widely credited with peacefully resolving a long-standing territorial dispute with Nigeria over the oil-rich Bakassi Peninsula. By accepting the 2002 International Court of Justice(ICJ) rulling and negotiating the 2026 Greentree-Agreement, he successfully averted war between the two nation.

  • Foreign Policy:

He diversified cameroon's international partnerships, maintaining strong historical ties with France while significantly expanding economic and diplomatic relations with china and other global powers.

2. Economy and Infrastructure

  • Economic Stabilization:

Early in his tenure, Biya steered the country through structural adjustment programs, laying the groundwork for macroeconomic stability and attracting foreign investment.

  • Major Energy Projects:

He oversaw the realization of major energy initiatives designed to secure the nation's power supply, including the construction of The Lom Pangar Dam and The Memve'ele Hydroelectric Plant.

  • Transportation & Ports:

His administration backed the expansion of crucial transport and trade infrastructure, notably the development of The Kribi Deep Seaport and the construction of the second bridge over the Wouri River in Douala.

3. Democratic and Institutional Reforms

  • Multiparty Democracy:

Biya transitioned Cameroon from a one-party state to a multiparty political system in 1990, legalizing opposition parties and creating a more pluralistic political environment.

  • Institutional Development:

Over the decades, his government has established key state institutions mandated by the constitution, including the Senate and the constitutional Council.

4. Education and Healthcare

  • University Expansion:

To increase higher education access, his administration created multiple state Universities across the country, expanding learning opportunities outside the capital.

  • Healthcare Upgrades:

Biya's tenure saw the construction and modernization of several major medical facilities, including the Yaounde Emergency Centre(CURY) and the Douala General Hospital, to improve access to specialize care.
